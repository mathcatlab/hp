Open Positions

For Everyone

We are excited to work with people who are interested in understanding the kinetics of catalytic reactions. The ideal applicant will be excited to work at the interface of experiments, applied mathematics, and data science, and energize the new research team. English will be the main langauge for scientific communication within our group. More details on mentoring policy and expectations can be found here.

Note: Hideshi will not check your CV or email unless it is clear that

  • you checked the mentoring policy above and
  • you are familiar with a minimum one of our research papers
We expect all lab members to be resourceful, so if you are serious about joining, please check online information sources.

If you are really motivated about joining our group, please

  • check what our lab is trying to accomplish
  • identify what is missing in the skillset of our current members
  • and demonstrate that you can fill the gap.

For Postdocs

All postdoc positions are filled at the moment of writing (June 2026), but we may have openings from April 2027 depending on the funding situation. The most reliable way to join us is to secure your own salary, such as through the scholarship programs listed below. The Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) is the biggest funder for postdoc scholarships in Japan. The details can be found here. This program is quite competitive, but is typically considered to be among the best postdoc positions in Japan in terms of financial support and prestige. Standard applications are due around April or May each year. There is another call usually around August for people coming from outside Japan. Please also consider the ICYS program, which is an institite-specific program for postdocs and independent researchers. Deadlines are usually in March and September.

For Engineers / Part-time Workers

We are currently accepting applications from people with experimental experience, especially if you can help us automate our lab. We may consider theoretical applicants with exceptional Python or Notion skills. Please contact me for details.

For PhD Candidates

We welcome PhD students already enrolled in a doctoral program in Japan. The most reliable funding route is the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) DC1/DC2 fellowship. Additionally, many university have specific programs to support PhD candidates. If you are already enrolled or planning to enroll in a Japanese PhD program and are interested in applying for DC1/DC2 with our lab as your host, please contact us to discuss.

For Undergraduates and Master Students

We are open to undergraduate and master students, but there is no fixed funding arrangement yet. This is decided case-by-case depending on the situation. Please contact us directly to discuss.
